# Sales SOP (Mock)

## Qualification checklist
1. Confirm business problem and urgency.
2. Confirm current workflow/system.
3. Confirm success metric for pilot.
4. Confirm buyer role and decision process.
5. Confirm budget range and timeline expectations.

## Follow-up principles
- Ask concise, business-oriented questions.
- Avoid technical over-commitments before discovery.
- Propose pilot scope only after data and system readiness are clarified.
- Escalate to human AE if legal/security/procurement constraints appear.
